Official atlas page:

Changes made for this web atlas:
- copied the original embedded-gzip Int16 CT and label-map NRRD files without
  changing voxel values or spatial metadata;
- selected 11 official abdominal surface models and converted their VTK
  geometry to a single gzip-compressed GLB while preserving source positions;
- applied new display materials, browser interaction metadata, and a common
  RAS-to-web-view transform to the CT plane and surfaces together;
- generated a manifest containing hashes, geometry, labels, and provenance;
- left the unknown source label value 699 unmapped and transparent.
